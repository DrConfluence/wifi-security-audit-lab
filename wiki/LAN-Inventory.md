# Authorized LAN Inventory

When connected to an authorized Wi-Fi network, the assessment can derive the local IPv4 network and perform host discovery.

## LAB-001 observed state

- SSID: Shubh
- Device IP: 172.22.25.69
- Target: 172.22.25.0/24
- Hosts observed: 2
- Additional observed host: 172.22.25.133

The LAN inventory layer is deliberately separated from wireless discovery.
