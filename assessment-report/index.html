SANITIZED CASE STUDY

Controlled Wi-Fi Assessment

A presentation-ready example of how a real engagement should be documented without publishing third-party network data.

1. Authorization

Written authorization reference: LAB-001 (example only).

Scope: controlled laboratory network owned by the assessor.

2. Discovery

The scanner records SSID, BSSID, capabilities, signal level, frequency, band, channel, timestamp, and a unique scan identifier.

3. Authorization verification

Observed networks are compared against the explicit allowlist. A visible network that is absent from the allowlist remains unauthorized for the purposes of the assessment.

4. Evidence

Scan ID:             20260825-LAB-001
Networks observed:  6
Authorized:         1
Out of scope:       5
Status:             PASSIVE DISCOVERY COMPLETE

5. Active testing boundary

No password guessing, credential extraction, deauthentication, authentication bypass, or unauthorized association is performed by this project.

Any further testing requires explicit written scope.

6. Finding format

Observation

What was actually observed.

Impact

Why the observation matters to the authorized environment.

Evidence

Timestamped, reproducible evidence supporting the finding.

Recommendation

A defensive remediation or control improvement.
